## Item A — paired proving benchmark, v9.1 vs v9.2 (Mac M5 Pro, reference machine)
- **Same design as v9.1's row 6** (`../../t2_circuit_fix/zkdac_v9_1/RESULTS.md`): `scripts/prove_bench.mjs 10` of each version.
  - Order: v9.1, v9.2, v9.1, v9.2.
  - D = 1..4, 10 fresh instances per D, seeds 1000..1009. The seeds are the same for both versions.
- **Conditions.** The machine runs nothing else from this project: no other experiment, build or smoke on the Mac during the four runs. This is checked with `pgrep` before and after each run.
- **Output:** `results/prove_bench_paired_v91_v92.txt`, with the four JSON outputs.
- **Expectation.** v9.2 adds one constraint (holder_sk ≠ 0). The per-D difference of the means is expected within the run-to-run variation of one version. Every proof verifies, 10 / 10 per D per run.
- **Report:** per D, the means of both runs of both versions, and the differences, compared with the run-to-run differences.
